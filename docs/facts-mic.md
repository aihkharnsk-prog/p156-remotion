# facts-mic — ไมค์ไร้สายพกพา แบบหนีบปกเสื้อ 2.4 GHz + ตัวรับ USB-C/Lightning (ยังไงนะ)

Facts only. No brand names in the script. Spec-sheet figures are used only as "typical / many models" ranges.

Sources (S#):
- S1 Wikipedia "Electret microphone" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electret_microphone) — permanent charge, diaphragm, JFET preamp, used in lavalier mics
- S2 eeNews Europe "MEMS microphone technology adapting to changing use cases" (https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/mems-microphone-technology-adapting-to-changing-use-cases/) — MEMS diaphragm + backplate → capacitance → ASIC → analog/digital (PDM, delta-sigma ADC)
- S3 Wikipedia "2.4 GHz radio use" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2.4_GHz_radio_use, read via mirror https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/2.4_GHz_radio_use) — devices sharing the band, microwave ovens, Bluetooth hopping, digital wireless mics
- S4 Network World "Wireless Wizards" (https://www.networkworld.com/article/869579/network-security-stop-the-interference.html) — microwave ovens do not frequency-hop, top 2.4 GHz interferers (2004 column)
- S5 Maono blog (https://www.maono.com/blogs/news/how-maono-pd200w-delivers-stable-interference-free-wireless-sound) — vendor description of frequency hopping + auto channel scan (marketing; its range numbers conflict, not used)
- S6 Eve Zone "2.4GHz wireless vs Bluetooth" (https://evezone.evetech.co.za/deep-dives/2-4ghz-wireless-vs-bluetooth-which-mode-should-you-use-for-gaming-audio) — 2.4 GHz dongle ~1–5 ms vs Bluetooth ~15–40 ms (gaming audio, not mics specifically)
- S7 EE Times Asia "Achieving Ultra-Low Latency Digital Wireless Audio" (https://www.eetasia.com/?p=53148) — Bluetooth Classic voice ~30 ms, LE Audio ~20–30 ms
- S8 Spec pages of creator-type 2.4 GHz lavalier kits: SmallRig Wave W1-C page (https://www.tanotis.com/collections/vendors/products/smallrig-wave-w1-c-wireless-lavalier-microphone-set-with-charging-case): latency <10 ms, 100 m line of sight, 5 h transmitter, case 730 mAh, USB-C; Saramonic Blink500 B5 page (https://www.musicworld.bg/en/c_1527/i_82262/Saramonic_Blink500_B5.html): Li-ion 3.7 V 400 mAh, ~5 h, voice delay ≤6 ms, 50 m open area / 30 m with obstacles, pairing buttons; Hollyland-type kit page (https://www.scmsinc.com/hollyland-lark-m2-with-lightning-plug-black-lark-m2-wireless-lavalier-microphone-with-lightning-plug-duo-shine-charcoal.html): ~10 h transmitter, case recharges transmitters twice, USB-C; BOYALINK page (https://boyamic.com/products/boyalink-all-in-one-design-wireless-microphone-system): 100 m open area, 10 h TX, 1000 mAh case recharges TX twice, USB-C + Lightning receivers, 48 kHz 16-bit, frequency hopping; Godox Virso M2 page (https://greentoe.com/product/Godox_Godox_Virso_M2_2-Person_Wireless_Microphone_System_for_Cameras_and_Smartphone---_VIRSO_M2): 13 h TX, case 3500 mAh, 2.4 GHz auto-scan channels
- S9 Eve Zone "Understanding AI noise canceling in modern wireless microphones" (https://evezone.evetech.co.za/quick-bytes/understanding-ai-noise-canceling-modern-wireless-microphones) — DSP chip in transmitter or receiver, speech/noise separation, over-processing sounds robotic, steady noise easiest
- S10 BGR "Foam microphone covers" (https://bgr.com/2113412/foam-microphone-covers-important-audio-purpose) — foam disperses air before diaphragm, ~8 dB wind reduction, fur 25–40 dB, dulls highs, blocks dust/saliva/moisture
- S11 Battery University BU-808 (https://batteryuniversity.com/article/how-to-prolong-lithium-based-batteries) — Li-ion aging, 300–500 cycles typical rating, heat/high charge voltage accelerate fade
- S12 arXiv paper: Pinson & Bazant, "Theory of SEI Formation in Rechargeable Batteries: Capacity Fade..." (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.3672) — SEI growth locks up lithium, fade, temperature-sensitive
- S13 Thomann "Microphone maintenance and care" (https://www.thomannmusic.com/onlineexpert_page_microphone_accessories_maintenance_and_care.html) — keep water out of capsule, windscreens/foam washable, check loose connectors, foam/elastics replaceable after years
