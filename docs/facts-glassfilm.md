# facts-glassfilm — ฟิล์มกระจกกันรอย 9H (ยังไงนะ)

Facts only. No brand names. Screen-protector glass is made by thermal tempering or by chemical (ion-exchange) strengthening depending on product; the script explains thermal tempering as the example and the compression-skin principle applies to both (Wikipedia mentions both).

Sources (S#): S1 Torras "What does 9H hardness mean" (https://glass.torraslife.com/blogs/news/what-does-9h-hardness-mean) · S2 Wikipedia "Tempered glass" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempered_glass) · S3 MagicJohn "How screen protectors absorb shock" (https://magicjohn.com/blogs/news/how-screen-protectors-absorb-shock-prevent-scratches, vendor marketing) · S4 Theodore Gray, PopSci tempered glass note (https://theodoregray.com/PeriodicTable/PopSci/2008/07/1/index.html) · S5 CalPoly Grand Ave housing blog "What is 9H surface hardness" (https://grandavehousing.calpoly.edu/news/what-is-9h-surface-hardness, NOT used: it equates 9H with Mohs 9, which S1 explicitly warns against)

| 6 | Thermal tempering: glass heated well above its glass transition (about 620 C), then cooled quickly with air; the surface contracts and solidifies first, the core later, leaving surface in compression and core in tension | S2, S4 | High |
| 7 | Compression at the surface keeps surface flaws from growing; tempered glass is about 4x stronger than annealed (not stated in script) | S2, S4 | High |
| 8 | When tempered glass breaks it crumbles to small pieces rather than large shards | S2, S4 | High |

| 10 | Silicone adhesive layer holds fragments and damps shock | S3 | Medium (vendor description) |
| 11 | Sharp concentrated point impacts can break tempered glass even mid-pane; pressure is greater per area for a point than for a flat object | S2 | High |
| 12 | Edges carry the highest tensile stress and are the likeliest failure point; corners concentrate drop force; rounded (2.5D/3D) edges are said to deflect it ("บางรุ่น") | S2, S3 | Medium-High (rounded edge benefit is vendor claim) |
| 13 | A cracked protector is a weak spot and absorbs less on the next impact | S1 (impact performance depends on edge stress etc.), S3 ("damaged protector can't absorb shock effectively") | Medium (vendor + reasoning) |
