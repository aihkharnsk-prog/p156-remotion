# facts-oxybleach — ผงขจัดคราบผ้า oxygen bleach (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 MEL Magazine "How do stain removers work" (https://melmagazine.com/en-us/story/how-do-stain-removers-work) · S2 CheckAppliance "What is oxygen bleach" (https://checkappliance.co.uk/what-is-oxygen-bleach) · S3 House Digest "Why storing OxiClean solution in a spray bottle is a bad idea" (https://housedigest.com/1570261/dangerous-reason-dont-store-oxiclean-solution-spray-bottle)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oxygen bleach = sodium percarbonate, essentially soda ash + hydrogen peroxide in one crystalline powder | S1, S2 | High |
| 2 | In water it dissolves, releasing hydrogen peroxide; soda ash softens water / makes it mildly alkaline, which helps other cleaning ingredients lift soil | S1 | High (alkaline = general chemistry) |
| 3 | Hydrogen peroxide breaks down into water and oxygen; the oxygen bubbles loosen the stain's grip on the fabric | S1 | High |
| 4 | Peroxide also chemically oxidises/decolourises stain molecules ("ย่อยโมเลกุลสีของคราบ"). S1 emphasises the bubble/lifting mechanism and does not mention oxidation; oxidation of chromophores is the usual chemistry explanation | general chemistry | Medium |
| 5 | Warm water speeds it up; one guide says it works at 40 C or above. Script: "หลายยี่ห้อแนะนำน้ำอุ่น ส่วนน้ำเย็นช้าลงมาก" (does not claim zero effect in cold water) | S2 | Medium |
| 6 | Safe for coloured fabrics, unlike chlorine bleach, but patch-test first | S1, S2 | High |
| 7 | Not for wool and silk (protein fibres): it cannot tell protein stain from protein fibre; check care label | S2 | High |
| 8 | Works well on organic stains (food, drinks, grass, sweat); may not be enough for grease or some synthetic stains | S1 | High |
| 9 | Solution loses effect within a few hours (one source says about six); mix only what you need and follow label soak time | S3, S2 | Medium-High |
| 10 | Do not seal a mixed solution in a closed container or spray bottle: oxygen gas builds pressure and the container can burst | S3 | High |
| 11 | Store powder dry and closed (activates when wet); avoid inhaling; keep from children | S2 | High |
| 12 | Nudge: write the date you opened the tub to learn how many months it lasts (no source) | n/a | n/a |

Animation notes: crystal lattice, H2O2/H2O/O2 molecules, fibre weave and bubble counts are schematic; the powder is drawn as a 1:1 pairing of H2O2 and Na2CO3 symbols for readability (real sodium percarbonate is 2Na2CO3·3H2O2). No product brand shown.

Not used because unverified: exact activation temperature for any brand, % of hydrogen peroxide, claim of killing germs, pH values.
