# facts-dehumid — ถุงดูดความชื้น (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 Oxy/Peladow calcium chloride product info sheet (https://prod-calciumchloride.oxy.com/siteassets/documents/product-info-sheets/peladow_dg_prodinfosheet.pdf) · S2 Clevast "How do moisture absorber bags work" (https://clevast.com/blogs/tips/how-do-moisture-absorber-bags-work-your-faqs-answered)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bags contain calcium chloride granules that attract water vapour from the air | S2 | High |
| 2 | Calcium chloride is hygroscopic: absorbs moisture from the air and can go all the way to liquid brine (deliquescent) | S1 | High |
| 3 | The granules gradually dissolve into liquid that drips into a lower chamber; no power needed | S2 | High |
| 4 | Fresh granules then keep absorbing; the collecting chamber is often transparent so you can see the water | S2 | Medium-High |
| 5 | Upper compartment wrapped in a vapour-permeable material/membrane, lower chamber stores liquid: common design, script says "หลายรุ่นออกแบบแบบนี้" | S2 (descriptions), general | Medium |
| 6 | Works best in small enclosed spaces (wardrobe, drawer) because humid air is not constantly replaced; large open rooms: one bag helps little (inference/standard guidance) | general | Medium-High |
| 7 | Low humidity: collection is slower and crystals may form instead of liquid (normal) | S2 | High |
| 8 | Liquid is concentrated brine: do not drink, keep from children/pets, avoid leaks onto valuables; brine can rust/corrode metals (general knowledge for CaCl2 brine; S2 only says keep away from valuables) | S2, general | Medium |
| 9 | Replacement: bags last roughly 60-110 days in one brand's high-humidity use; S2 gives no explicit "replace when" signal. Script uses the usual practical sign: lower chamber full and granules mostly gone | S2, general | Medium |
| 10 | Water vapour in humid air -> musty smell and mould on clothes | general | Medium-High |
| 11 | Nudge: when the bag is full of water it is time to replace it | n/a | n/a |

Animation notes: granule lattice (alternating Ca2+ / Cl-) is schematic (real CaCl2 has 1 Ca per 2 Cl); bag cutaway and water molecules not to scale; wardrobe scene illustrative.

Not used because unverified: absorbed volume per bag, exact relative humidity threshold for deliquescence (S1 did not state one), disposal instructions (varies by brand).
