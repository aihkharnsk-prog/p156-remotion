# facts-softener — น้ำยาปรับผ้านุ่ม (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 Science ABC, how fabric softeners work (https://www.scienceabc.com/eyeopeners/how-do-fabric-softeners-work) · S2 general surfactant chemistry (cationic quats, anionic detergents) · S3 Wikipedia "Fabric softener" (search result only; page could not be fetched in this session)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Softener coats fabric with a thin layer of positively charged quaternary ammonium compounds ("quats") | S1 | High |
| 2 | Cotton fibres carry negative surface charge in water; the positive heads bind to them | S1, S2 | High |
| 3 | Dry fibres rubbing directly feel harsh/stiff | S1 (softeners make fabric softer, easier to iron), general | Medium (qualitative) |
| 4 | Quat = positive head + long hydrocarbon (fatty) tail that repels water | S1 ("water-repelling tails"), S2 | High |
| 5 | Because tails are hydrophobic the compounds do not dissolve well; they disperse as small vesicles (milky liquid) | S2 | Medium (standard colloid chemistry; no fetched source, drawn schematically) |
| 6 | Tails sticking out form a lubricating film so fibres slide past each other; fabric feels softer and is easier to iron | S1 | High |
| 7 | Positive charge cancels static cling; perfume is carried onto the fabric | S1 | High ("กลิ่นติดผ้าได้นาน" is a mild extension, not sourced) |
| 8 | Rinse step: molecules on the fibres stay while free ones are drained | S2 | Medium (schematic) |
| 9 | Downside: water-repelling tails reduce absorbency of towels and diapers; script says "มักมีคำแนะนำให้ระวัง" | S1 | High (S1 states the reduced absorbency; the advice wording is generic) |
| 10 | Added in the final rinse; detergents are mostly anionic and can bind with cationic quats, so they should not be present together | S1 (final rinse), S2 | Medium (the reason is standard chemistry, not in S1) |
| 11 | Nudge: lift the bottle, if light then rebuy | n/a | n/a |

Animation notes: molecule sizes, number of molecules per fibre and the "vesicle" circles are schematic. Real fibres are far larger than a molecule; the view is a conceptual close-up.

Not used because unverified: softener buildup over many washes, effects on flame-retardant fabrics, concentrations, plant-based softener chemistry (esterquats vs old quats), dryer-sheet mechanism.
