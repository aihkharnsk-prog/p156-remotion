# facts-humidifier — เครื่องพ่นไอน้ำอัลตราโซนิก (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 InstaCuppa "Ultrasonic humidifier: what it means and how it works" (https://instacuppastore.com/blogs/main-page/ultrasonic-humidifier-what-it-means-how-it-works) · S2 HVAC Training Shop "How do ultrasonic humidifiers work" (https://hvactrainingshop.com/how-do-ultrasonic-humidifiers-work/)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Small piezoelectric disc (ceramic/metal) at tank bottom converts electricity to vibration | S1, S2 | High |
| 2 | Vibrates at ultrasonic frequency, 1.7 MHz cited (some units ~2.4 MHz); ~85x above hearing limit so nearly silent. Script: 'หลายรุ่นราวหนึ่งจุดเจ็ดล้านครั้งต่อวินาที' | S1 | Medium-High (one source; model-dependent) |
| 3 | Droplets about 1-5 microns, smaller than a red blood cell | S1 | Medium-High |
| 4 | Mechanism of mist: surface ripples (capillary waves) whose crests break off as droplets; S1/S2 only say vibration 'creates microscopic droplets' | general physics (ultrasonic atomisation) | Medium |
| 5 | Water is not heated, so mist is cool; mist is liquid droplets that then evaporate (script: ไม่ใช่ไอน้ำ) | general; S2 'cool/vibrating diaphragm' | High |
| 6 | Dissolved Ca/Mg minerals leave with the mist as white dust; mineral build-up damages/degrades performance | S1, S2 | High (S1 white dust, S2 mineral deposits) |
| 7 | Scale on the disc itself reduces vibration/mist: S1 does not state it directly (it describes tank buildup); consistent with S2 'deposits can stop unit working' | S1, S2 | Medium |
| 8 | Cleaning: vinegar soak ~30 min, soft brush on disc, rinse, distilled water; empty old water | S1, S2 | High (S1 gives inconsistent vinegar ratios, so none stated) |
| 9 | Nudge: look at the disc for white deposit | n/a | n/a |

Animation notes: disc vibration is shown slowed; wave amplitude, droplet counts and the hygrometer are schematic. Not used: exact droplet physics, humidity numbers, vinegar ratio, cleaning intervals (S1 contradicts itself).
