# facts-bagsealer — เครื่องซีลถุงมินิ (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 US Packaging & Wrapping "How does a heat sealer work" (https://uspackagingandwrapping.com/blog/how-does-a-heat-sealer-work.html) · S2 Wikipedia (via Wikiwand) "Heat seal" (https://www.wikiwand.com/en/articles/Heat_seal)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heat sealing joins thermoplastics with heat and pressure; quality depends on time, temperature, pressure | S2 | High |
| 2 | Impulse sealing: nichrome element heated only while current flows; wire heats and melts the film; pressure joins the sides | S1, S2 | High (for impulse sealers; mini handheld units are presumed similar) |
| 3 | Mini consumer sealers specifically use a nichrome wire: not confirmed per model; script says 'ส่วนใหญ่ ... เช่นนิกเกิลโครเมียม' | S1, S2 (generic sealers) | Medium |
| 4 | Clamp/cool: jaws hold the material while it cools so the seal can set before stress | S2 | High |
| 5 | Polymer chains of the two molten layers interdiffuse, then freeze (standard polymer science; not stated in S1/S2) | general | Medium-High |
| 6 | Too long -> melt-through hole, too short -> weak seal (practical knowledge, not verbatim in sources) | general | Medium |
| 7 | Only bags with a meltable (e.g. polyethylene) inner layer seal; plain paper does not. S2 does not mention polyethylene | general | Medium-High |
| 8 | Wire wear from repeated heat/cool cycles and plastic residue; no source verified | general | Medium (mechanism plausible, not sourced) |
| 9 | Nudge: check wire for thinning/breaks when seal is incomplete | n/a | n/a |

Animation notes: layer thicknesses and molecular chains are schematic; no temperatures or times are claimed. Not used: seal temperature for PE (no verified figure), button-hold times, battery vs USB power in mini units.
