# facts-toiletfoam — โฟมล้างห้องน้ำไม่ต้องขัด (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 US5678765 "Foam/spray nozzle assembly for trigger sprayer" (https://patents.google.com/patent/US5678765) · S2 arXiv 1912.07869 "Foamability of aqueous solutions: role of surfactant type and concentration" (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.07869) · S3 Science ABC "How does soap/detergent work" (https://www.scienceabc.com/pure-sciences/how-soap-detergent-work-saponification-hydrophobic-hydrophilic-dirty.html)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foam nozzle: liquid pumped through a swirl chamber, exits a small orifice as a divergent spray cone | S1 | High |
| 2 | Air is aspirated through openings beside the nozzle element during pumping; spray droplets hit the inner wall of a turbulence chamber and mix with air to form foam | S1 | High |
| 3 | Foam then passes through a perforated wall/grate (patent: moulded ribs, not a mesh) that adds more turbulence and air mixing. Script says "ตะแกรงซี่ถี่ๆ ... ฟองเล็กละเอียด"; "finer, more even bubbles" is the usual purpose but the patent text only says more mixing | S1 | Medium-High |
| 4 | Surfactants adsorb at the new air-water surfaces, lower surface tension, and slow thinning/rupture of the thin liquid films so foam persists | S2 | High |
| 5 | Surfactant molecules are amphipathic: hydrophilic head in water, hydrophobic tail prefers grease; tails surround oily dirt and heads face water so it can be rinsed away | S3 | High |
| 6 | Thick foam runs down slowly so it stays on sloped bowl walls and gives the cleaner more contact time | general reasoning / manufacturer claims, no source opened | Medium |
| 7 | "Rely on time not scrubbing" matches product-label dwell time; formula-specific | general | Medium |
| 8 | Surfactants do not dissolve limescale (needs acid); stubborn crust still needs a brush or an acid descaler | general chemistry | High |
| 9 | Nudge: thin foam or air-only spray = bottle nearly empty (observation, formula/pump dependent) | n/a | Medium |
