# facts-tissue — ทิชชู่ซอฟต์แพ็ค (ยังไงนะ)

Sources (S#): S1 US6053357 "Pop-up tissue and sheet dispenser" (https://patents.google.com/patent/US6053357) · S2 howeverythingworks.org "How does a paper towel absorb water?" (https://howeverythingworks.org/?p=1746) · S3 Science ABC "Why is wet paper so weak" (https://www.scienceabc.com/eyeopeners/why-is-wet-paper-so-weak-and-easy-to-tear.html)

| # | Claim in script | Source(s) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pop-up tissues are interfolded sheets stacked in a clip/carton; removing one sheet partly pulls the next adjacent sheet through the opening | S1 | High |
| 2 | The partly pulled sheet is held in a popped-up position by the edges of the slit until the user takes it | S1 (about a carton/clip slit; soft-pack film opening works the same way in principle) | Medium-High |
| 3 | "Fold of sheet hooks the tip of the next sheet" (nesting tip-in-fold, chain reaction sheet after sheet) is the standard explanation of interfolding; the patent states the result (next sheet pulled) but does not draw the fold-by-fold hook in these words | S1 + general product knowledge | Medium |
| 4 | Pulling hard/fast can bring two sheets or tear one (patent discusses tearing from friction/pressure against the container and limiting how many sheets come through the opening). Script says only "บางทีก็ติดมาสองแผ่นหรือขาด ค่อยๆ ดึงเรียบร้อยกว่า" | S1 | Medium |
| 5 | Paper towel/tissue = finely divided cellulose fibres; cellulose has many hydroxyl (-OH) groups that bond strongly with water | S2 | High |
| 6 | Water is drawn along the tiny gaps between fibres (capillary wicking) | general physics; S2 did NOT mention capillary action | Medium (no source opened for this) |
| 7 | Wet paper is weak because water disrupts the fibre-to-fibre hydrogen bonds (water bonds to cellulose instead); fibres swell and slide; strength returns on drying | S3 | High |
| 8 | Nudge: runs out fast / keep a spare pack (no source needed) | n/a | n/a |
